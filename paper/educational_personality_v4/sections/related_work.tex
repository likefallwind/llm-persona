\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{Personality measurement and behavioral consistency.}
Psychometric studies assess personality expression and its control through
prompts \citep{serapio2025psychometric}; the Trait of AI Testbench (TRAIT) uses
scenario-based choices \citep{lee2025trait}. Yet self-reports do not reliably
predict behavior in some tests \citep{han2026illusion}, and their predictive
value depends on the behavior and conversational context
\citep{kocielnik2026rethinking}. Consistency in following an assigned persona also varies across
tasks \citep{reusens2025consistency}. Behavioral Mode Axes studies observable
choices across interaction contexts and controls them through activation
steering \citep{liu2026bma}. We examine recurring tendencies in teaching
responses and test when past behavior helps predict new responses.

\paragraph{Teaching styles and adaptation.}
A comparison of tutoring dialogues finds differences in teaching actions and
response complexity across three large language models (LLMs)
\citep{kucheria2025cima}. Tests that remove information about students show that models differ in how
they adapt their teaching
\citep{borchers2025adaptivity}. Steering methods learn teaching styles
from human dialogues \citep{lee2026tutorpersonas}, while Personality-Aware
Teaching Strategies (PATS) adapts teaching
strategies to simulated student personalities \citep{rooein2026pats}.
Our focus is the models' own teaching tendencies: we test their predictive
value on new inputs and how shared instructions reshape them.

\paragraph{Educational benchmarks and our starting point.}
MRBench provides human ratings across pedagogical dimensions
\citep{maurya2025mrbench}, and MathTutorBench evaluates multiple open-ended
tutoring capabilities \citep{macina2025mathtutorbench}. TutorBench assesses
explanations, feedback, and hints with example-specific rubrics
\citep{srinivasa2025tutorbench}. LongTutor evaluates use of learning histories
for diagnosis and adaptive teaching \citep{li2026longtutor}.
Our investigation began with archived evaluations across these and other
educational benchmarks (Appendix~\ref{app:archive-scope}). We analyze relevant
subsets for recurring behavior, then test candidate tendencies in controlled
experiments. This complements teaching-quality evaluation with evidence about
which behaviors remain predictable and how instructions change them.
