\section*{Ethical Considerations}
The controlled experiments use student messages and problems written by
research agents, without recruiting learners. Behavioral profiles describe model
outputs; they should not be used to diagnose students' emotions or infer human
psychological traits in a model. Favorable-looking teaching habits do not
establish correctness, learning benefit, or suitability for every learner.
Applying profiles beyond the tested language, tasks, and models could
mislead educators or overlook needs absent from the evaluation. Educational
use requires checking the intended activity and population; our harness
implications remain proposals for evaluation.
