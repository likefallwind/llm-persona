## Educational interpretations and configuration predictions

1. **Supported interpretation:** predictable teaching tendencies support informed expectations alongside capability. Describe each behavior and its tested scope; neither every reply nor every candidate dimension is stable. Educational effectiveness is not ranked by these profiles.
2. **Supported interpretation:** instruction control does not determine the entire teaching response. Required actions can converge while other actions remain different; this does not establish preservation of the original style or an internal causal mechanism. Evaluate the full set of activity requirements, including constraints such as withholding the answer.
3. **Motivated development principle:** evaluate defaults, persistence, and instruction responses together to identify model-specific gaps relative to the activity, guide Harness configuration, and validate the configured system. Reusable mechanisms need evaluation with the supported model. This is not a validated selection procedure; Harness benefits, universal necessity, optimal configurations, and learning gains remain untested.
