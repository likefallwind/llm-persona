## Empirical answer to the three questions

1. **Different defaults, uneven repetition.** Canonical profiles differ across
   deployments, but not every pair: DeepSeek and Doubao both reveal answers in
   every canonical response. M3's elicitation repeat agreement is 57.8%, while
   the rare DeepSeek/Doubao elicitation positives have zero positive repeat
   agreement despite high overall agreement. Predictability concerns action
   distributions, not identical actions in every request.
2. **Prospective default prediction and selective conditional prediction.**
   All three default Brier gains pass the six-test Holm rule: revelation
   .091275, elicitation .129698 and acknowledgement .019821. Student-state
   increments beyond model-by-domain profiles are .000431, -.000163 and
   .009734. Only acknowledgement has a resolved positive increment. The last
   comparison concerns scripted cues and the chosen predictor class; it does
   not establish or disprove all adaptability.
3. **Convergence on demanded actions, residual permissible differences.**
   On the same sixteen sources, ASK produces elicitation in every response
   from every deployment. EXPLAIN produces answer revelation in every response.
   EXPLAIN explicitly permits follow-up reasoning: GLM and M3 still elicit in
   62.5% and 45.3% of responses versus 1.6–5.5% for the other three. These are
   co-occurring actions, not opposite personality poles or evidence of their
   temporal order. Neutral B moves GLM revelation and acknowledgement by
   15.6 percentage points on the observed sample; no neutral comparison meets
   the conservative population-equivalence criterion.

## Results that constrain the thesis

- Removing GLM attenuates default revelation and elicitation gains to .003942
  and .030800. Removing M2.7 leaves acknowledgement default gain .001748 with
  a source-bootstrap interval spanning zero. The full-panel result cannot be
  generalized to equal effects across all model subsets or pairs.
- Eight coding panels preserve the broad default/conditional distinction,
  while 200 source resamples show training-set sensitivity. These checks reuse
  responses and do not count as independent replications.
- The missing-code enumeration leaves all six primary estimates and adjusted
  p-values exactly unchanged for either binary value of the affected canonical
  GLM codes. This does not validate coding stability across the Gateway outage.
- All canonical information-request rates are zero, but withholding necessary
  facts in eight opportunity sources elicits requests at different rates.
  Absence without opportunity is not a stable trait estimate. Some secondary
  events have weak positive coder agreement and remain explicitly secondary.
- All conclusions concern five observed deployments, English agent-authored
  inputs and new source problems within four known domains. They do not
  establish human-equivalent traits, education-specific internal mechanisms,
  learner understanding, empathy or learning gain.
